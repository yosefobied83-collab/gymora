# GYMORA Nutrition Method

The nutrition feature is a general adult (18+) planning aid, not a medical diet.

- Resting energy: Mifflin-St Jeor predictive equation.
- Activity: user-selected activity multiplier.
- Goal adjustment: moderate multiplier around estimated daily energy (bulk +8%, cut -10%, strength +5%, fitness maintenance).
- Protein defaults: 1.4–1.8 g/kg depending on training goal.
- Fat target: ~25% of estimated calories; carbohydrate fills the remaining estimated calories.
- Meals are generated only from foods the user marks as available.
- All food calories/macros are approximate example-serving values and can vary with brand/preparation.

Safety: users with medical conditions, pregnancy, kidney disease, eating disorders, or therapeutic nutrition needs are directed to a qualified professional instead of this general tool.
